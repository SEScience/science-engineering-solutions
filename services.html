Products & Services

Technical Consulting

We provide specialized technical consulting services to support water and wastewater utilities, research institutions, and analytical laboratories in understanding microplastic contamination, optimizing treatment processes, and preparing for emerging regulatory requirements.

Sampling Plan Development

We design scientifically robust sampling plans tailored to specific water matrices — including drinking water, wastewater, stormwater, and environmental systems — ensuring accurate, representative, and defensible microplastic measurements.

Analytical Method Development for Microplastics Quantification

Our team develops and refines advanced analytical methodologies for micro- and nanoplastics quantification, including polymer identification and size distribution analysis. We utilize state-of-the-art instrumentation and rigorous QA/QC protocols to ensure data reliability and regulatory readiness.

Enhancing Microplastic Removal in Water Treatment

We collaborate with water treatment facilities to evaluate and optimize existing treatment processes such as filtration systems, membrane technologies, and advanced oxidation processes to improve microplastic removal efficiency and resolve performance challenges.

Enhancing Microplastic Removal in Wastewater Reuse Systems

Our consulting services support potable reuse and wastewater treatment facilities in assessing microplastic fate and transport across treatment trains, identifying critical control points, and implementing strategies to strengthen removal performance and meet future regulatory standards.

Education & Training

We offer customized, hands-on training programs as well as regularly scheduled online workshops covering the full spectrum of micro- and nanoplastics quantification and analysis. Our programs are designed for water and wastewater utilities, analytical laboratories, research groups, and environmental professionals seeking to build technical capacity in sampling, extraction, and advanced characterization techniques.
