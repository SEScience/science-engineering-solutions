Executive Leadership

Dr. Maryam Salehi

President & CEO

Dr. Maryam Salehi is an environmental engineer and scientist with extensive experience in drinking water treatment, microplastics, nanoplastics, environmental contaminants and analytical chemistry. Her research focuses on the occurrence, fate, transport and removal of microplastics in aquatic systems while developing innovative technologies for environmental monitoring and treatment optimization. She has collaborated with universities, utilities and research institutions to advance scientific understanding and practical engineering solutions.

Dr. Alexander Ccanccapa-Cartagena

Chief Scientific Officer (CSO)

Dr. Alexander Ccanccapa-Cartagena specializes in analytical chemistry, mass spectrometry and environmental toxicology with more than ten years of experience developing analytical methods for environmental, pharmaceutical and biological applications. His expertise includes LC-MS/MS, LC-QTOF, Py-GC/MS, ICP-OES, FTIR, laboratory method validation, QA/QC and scientific consulting for industry and research laboratories.
