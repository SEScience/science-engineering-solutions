About SES

Bridging Scientific Innovation and Practical Environmental Solutions

Our Story

With more than a decade of academic research in microplastics, we witnessed firsthand the growing challenges faced by industry and by water and wastewater utilities in addressing microplastic pollution and its potential impacts on water and food safety.

As regulatory interest intensified and the need for reliable analytical methods increased, it became clear that a gap existed between cutting-edge scientific knowledge and the practical tools available to environmental professionals and industries.

Our story began with a simple goal: to bridge that gap. Motivated by years of research experience and a commitment to improving environmental and public health, we founded this LLC to provide specialized technical support, advanced analytical solutions, and targeted training for organizations preparing for upcoming regulations related to microplastic monitoring in drinking water.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower water and wastewater utilities, laboratories, and research groups with accurate data, scientifically sound methodologies, and practical strategies to understand and reduce microplastics in the environment.

We strive to advance high-quality, transparent, and reliable analytics that drive informed decision-making and support compliance with emerging standards.

Our Values

Our values are built on scientific integrity, innovation, collaboration, and service. We believe in delivering evidence-based solutions, cultivating strong partnerships with our clients, and contributing to a cleaner, safer, and more resilient environment.

Through our work, we aim to make advanced microplastics research accessible and actionable for the organizations shaping the future of water quality and environmental protection.
