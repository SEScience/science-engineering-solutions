About SES

Transforming Scientific Knowledge into Practical Solutions

Our Story

Science & Engineering Solutions, LLC (SES) was founded to bridge the gap between advanced scientific research and real-world engineering solutions. Years of research in environmental contaminants, microplastics, analytical chemistry and laboratory method development demonstrated the need for specialized technical support capable of translating cutting-edge science into practical solutions for industry, government agencies and research institutions.


Today, SES combines scientific excellence with practical engineering experience to provide reliable analytical services, consulting, laboratory support and research collaboration.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver high-quality scientific consulting, analytical methodologies and innovative engineering solutions that help laboratories, industries and water utilities solve complex environmental and analytical challenges.

Our Vision

To become a recognized scientific consulting company supporting organizations worldwide through innovation, scientific integrity and advanced analytical expertise.
